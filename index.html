TIDES payout system OCEAN
Rolling 8-block window: every share tracked individually across the last 8 blocks of network difficulty. Your cut of each block is exact and auditable.
Each share paid ~8× on average: shares never dropped early — credited across multiple block finds, reducing variance far below standard PPLNS.
Non-custodial direct payment: BTC paid directly from the coinbase transaction to your wallet. OCEAN never holds your funds.
Full tx fees included: unlike FPPS (which estimates fees conservatively), TIDES passes 100% of actual fee revenue. During fee spikes, TIDES can significantly outperform FPPS pools.
Payout variance by method
FPPS (Foundry, F2Pool) — lowest variance, estimated fees only
TIDES (OCEAN) — low variance, actual full fees passed through
PPLNS (AntPool, ViaBTC) — moderate variance
Solo mining — extreme variance (years between blocks at 1 PH/s)

Network governance — Bitcoin Knots distribution & BIP-110 signaling
Bitcoin Knots node distribution LIVE · bitnodes.io
Bitcoin Knots is an alternative full-node implementation by Luke Dashjr (also OCEAN's co-founder). It enforces stricter transaction relay policies — including an 83-byte OP_RETURN cap and spam filters — and is the primary driver behind BIP-110 adoption. Running Knots is a policy vote: operators choose which transactions their node relays.
